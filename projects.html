01 / HOUSING

Housie.

Finding a home involves two connected decisions: where to live and who to live with. Housie brings people, preferences, and housing into one shared search.

I built the iOS app, backend, and voice agent. My co-founder and I are preparing for a pilot.

02 / COMMERCE

StealOrSus.

A shopping check for people and their AI agents. It brings evidence about a deal together before someone decides to buy.

The project explores price, total cost, listing photos, and product recalls as parts of a more informed shopping decision.

03 / REAL ESTATE

PropLeads.

My first real-estate build matched home buyers in Hyderabad to property listings based on their intent.

The project explored how to make property discovery more relevant to what a buyer is actually looking for.
